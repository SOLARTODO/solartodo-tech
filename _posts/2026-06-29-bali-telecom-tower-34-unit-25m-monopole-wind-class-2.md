## 25m Urban Macro Sites for Bali Coastal Load

A 34-site infill package in Bali can be specified around **25m tapered Q345 steel monopoles**, with each structure weighing about **9t** and designed for **50m/s basic wind speed** under a **TIA-222-H wind class 2 approach using a 1.15 factor**. This configuration targets urban macro coverage and capacity in dense coastal corridors, airport access roads, hotel districts, beaches, and Denpasar/Sarbagita demand clusters.

Bali’s network planning case is not only about basic geographic coverage. BPS 2025 data gives Bali Province an estimated mid-2024 population of **4,461,260** across roughly **5,780 km2**, equal to more than **770 people per km2**. Tourism traffic increases the mobile load profile: reported 2025 foreign arrivals reached **6,948,754**, a **9.72%** increase from 2024. For an island with concentrated visitor movement, hospitality operations, navigation demand, logistics, and emergency communication requirements, the practical design question is how to add capacity without deploying excessive tower height or footprint.

Global demand indicators support the same capacity-led framing. ITU 2025 figures place worldwide internet users at about **6 billion**, while **5G subscriptions** are around **3 billion** and **5G population coverage** is about **55%**. Indonesia’s telecom indicators also show more than **120 mobile-cellular subscriptions per 100 people** in recent data series, reflecting multi-SIM and device-heavy behavior. For Bali, these figures point toward densification through urban macro infill rather than a first-coverage buildout.

## Core Tower Specification

The recommended SOLARTODO configuration uses **approximately 34 units** of **25m tapered round or octagonal steel monopole towers**. The height sits at the upper end of the **15-25m urban infill class**, which is normally associated with rooftop or constrained urban deployment, **1 platform**, **3-6 panel antennas**, and an **8-15t** structural weight range. At about **9t per tower**, or roughly **350kg/m**, the proposed structure remains inside that class, subject to final structural calculation and site-specific loading confirmation.

| Parameter | Engineering Value | Planning Note |
|---|---:|---|
| Deployment quantity | ~34 towers | Island-scale urban macro infill package |
| Tower height | 25m | Upper end of 15-25m urban infill class |
| Steel grade and form | Q345 tapered round or octagonal monopole | Hot-dip galvanized finish for coastal exposure |
| Approx. tower weight | ~9t, ~350kg/m | Within 8-15t class for this height band |
| Wind basis | 50m/s basic wind speed, 1.15 factor | Wind class 2 under TIA-222-H methodology |
| Typical equipment load | 3 panel antennas, 1 microwave dish, RRU, 1 small cell | Per-site active and backhaul equipment set |

Each site should be engineered for **3 panel antennas**, **1 microwave dish**, RRU equipment, and **1 small cell**. That antenna schedule is consistent with an urban macro role where the tower supports sectorized radio coverage plus microwave backhaul or interconnection. Final checks should include antenna effective projected area, mounting elevations, service cable routing, ladder and platform loads, foundation reactions, and grounding continuity.

## Coastal Materials, Logistics, and Deployment Timing

Bali’s environment requires corrosion planning. The relevant exposure conditions are tropical heat, seasonal rainfall, coastal humidity, and salt-bearing air near marine corridors. For this reason, **hot-dip galvanized Q345 steel**, sealed cable routes, dependable earthing, and medium corrosion detailing are appropriate for a **30-year design life**. The corrosion package should be treated as part of the structural specification, not as an accessory item added after tower sizing.

For shipping and site access, a **CKD sectional format** is suitable because it can reduce transport volume by **60-70%** compared with larger preassembled sections. That matters for Bali port handling, urban road movement, and crane planning at constrained sites. Procurement sequencing should allow **30-45 days for production before shipment**, followed by foundation construction, tower erection, equipment installation, alignment, grounding verification, and commissioning.

For engineering teams comparing monopole, rooftop, and heavier lattice options, the 25m monopole profile gives a compact structure with defined equipment capacity, moderate tower mass, and manageable logistics.
