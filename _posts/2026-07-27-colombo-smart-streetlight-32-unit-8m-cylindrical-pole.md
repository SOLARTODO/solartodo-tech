## 32 Poles for an 800 m Colombo Corridor

A corridor package using **32 smart streetlight poles at 25 m spacing** covers approximately **800 m** of dense urban frontage. In Colombo, that spacing is relevant because transport, retail, tourism, municipal services, pedestrian movement, and curbside vehicle activity are concentrated inside a municipal area of about **37 km²**. The Colombo Municipal Council resident base was about **561,000 people in 2012**, while the broader Colombo District exceeded **2.3 million**, so equipment density is driven by daily corridor load rather than only resident population.

The reference configuration uses an **8 m seamless cylindrical pole** with **Ø219 mm diameter** and **5 mm wall thickness**. The finish is **matte white RAL9003**, and the lighting module is a **100 W multi-ring glow luminaire** producing **15,000 lm**. Across 32 units, installed lighting output reaches **480,000 lm** before dimming logic or operating schedules are applied.

|---|---:|---:|
| Pole geometry | 8 m height, Ø219 mm, 5 mm wall | 32 poles over about 800 m |
| Lighting | 100 W, 15,000 lm | 3,200 W, 480,000 lm |
| EV charging | 11 kW AC Type 2 | 32 AC charge points |
| Solar wrap and storage | 160 W CIGS, 3,000 Wh LFP | 5.12 kW solar wrap, 96,000 Wh storage |

## Integrated Power, Charging, and Backup

Each pole combines grid-connected street lighting with local backup energy. The wrapped photovoltaic section is specified as **CIGS thin-film** positioned from roughly **6.5 m to 7.3 m** on the shaft, with about **160 W** generation capacity per pole. This is paired with a **3,000 Wh LFP battery** and **MPPT** control for backup operation of sensors, communications, display, and emergency functions. The configuration is not framed as a fully off-grid road-lighting system. It is better understood as a grid-connected urban asset with distributed reserve capacity.

The EV charging package remains inside the cylindrical pole envelope. Each unit includes a **flush 11 kW AC Type 2 charger**, **5 m coiled cable**, **flush touchscreen at 1.5 m**, and **dual USB-A ports** without increasing the pole base beyond **Ø219 mm**. For procurement review, the important point is that charging, interaction, and auxiliary power are integrated into a narrow roadside profile rather than mounted as separate external cabinets.

The phrase **cylindrical Ø219mm flush-integrated pole** is technically accurate for this profile because the charger, touchscreen, cable handling, communications, and sensor interfaces are specified as embedded elements rather than bracketed add-ons. That reduces exposed attachment points, which matters in Colombo’s warm and humid coastal setting where typical temperatures sit around **26°C to 31°C** and rainfall conditions require attention to sealing and corrosion control.

## Corridor-Level Equipment Yield

At the 800 m scale, the package supplies **32 panoramic cameras**, **32 environmental sensing nodes**, **32 EV charge points**, and a lighting system based on **150 lm/W LED efficacy**. Smart dimming can reduce lighting electricity use compared with older HID operation, while LED service planning can extend toward **50,000 hours** when luminaires are designed around applicable **IEC 60598** safety and construction requirements.

For a B2B review, SOLARTODO would treat this as a corridor infrastructure bill of materials rather than a single-purpose lighting upgrade. The measurable outputs are pole count, spacing, installed lumens, AC charging capacity, backup watt-hours, sensor node count, and the mechanical envelope required to fit those functions into an **8 m Ø219 mm** streetscape asset.
